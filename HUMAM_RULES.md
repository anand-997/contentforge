# Rules Before Posting

Five rules everyone should follow before publishing.

Reference: [chapter_04_AI_Agents_n8n](https://github.com/PramodDutta/AITesterBlueprint3x/tree/main/chapter_04_AI_Agents_n8n)

1. **Know your audience.** Imagine that your managers and your company's people
   are reading your posts. Make sure the content is something you'd be proud to
   have them see — blog-worthy and professional.

2. **Make it your own.** Change the brand voice and rewrite the content in your
   own words. Never mention or promote the testing academy, because this is your
   own content.

3. **Always review.** Before posting, review your work. If you're not getting
   enough likes or comments, rewrite it in a more humanized, relatable way.

4. **Engage with others.** Comment on other people's posts, add your reviews,
   and like their content.

5. **Target and tag.** Find good HRs or good companies you want to reach out to.
   Tag them when possible, or share genuine reviews about what they're doing
   well. For example, if you're targeting BrowserStack, talk about why
   BrowserStack is great and how you use it as a case study. You can publish
   these posts and tag their HRs or their people — that works well too.
